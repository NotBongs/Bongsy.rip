How To Use This Every Payday
1 Enter your take-home paycheck.
2 Enter your current credit card balance.
3 Pay the credit card first.
4 Transfer each amount above to the matching bucket. Use ✎ Edit Buckets to set your own name, icon, color and percentage, or mark one bucket to auto-absorb whatever's left.
5 Use 🔗 Share / Import to copy a link with your exact bucket setup, or paste one someone sent you to load theirs.
Hands-off buckets (investments, crypto, etc.) work best when you don't check them daily and don't sell on a dip or a spike. Let the split do the work.
